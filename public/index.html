How these numbers are calculated

Contained
The conversation never reached a queue or an agent after the bot session. When Genesys has no conversation detail for a session, a disconnect inside the bot counts as contained and an exit back to the flow counts as escalated.
Reached an agent
An agent connected to the conversation that included the bot session.
Query self-service rate
Customer questions the bot marked as self-served, divided by all questions it handled (Genesys oBotSessionQuerySelfServed / oBotSessionQuery).
Intent recognized
Sessions where the bot matched at least one intent.
Bot response time
For each customer turn, the time from when Genesys captured the customer's input until the bot had its reply ready. The greeting is not counted. Median and longest are taken across every turn in the selection, so they apply per org, per virtual agent or per session depending on the filters. Genesys appears to keep turn-level data for only about the last 10 days, and sessions where the customer never replied have no turn to time, so these figures cover only the sessions that have turn data.
Recognition failure
Sessions with at least one no-match or no-input event.
